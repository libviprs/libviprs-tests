test(ported): follow JPEG XL encode to a real lossless codec

libviprs#619 and #620 give JPEG XL a decoder and a lossless encoder, so the
deferred contract test_jxlsave pinned, encode_jxl returning a typed
EncodeError::Unsupported, no longer holds. The signature moves too:
encode_jxl(lossless: bool) becomes encode_jxl(jxl::SaveOptions), so this is a
compile fix rather than only a behaviour follow-up.

The cell now does what the libvips cell it was ported from does, minus the two
steps that have nothing behind them. Steps 2 and 4 there encode lossy and
compare the buffer sizes; the only JPEG XL encoder reachable in pure Rust is
lossless modular with no VarDCT path anywhere in it, so distance, Q, tier and
effort have no spelling at all and there is no lossy buffer to size. That is
the deliberate answer #620 took rather than accepting one of those arguments
and discarding it.

Step 3 needs no tolerance, because there is no quantisation in the pipeline and
the round trip is the exact identity. The cell checks it at both integer
carriers, since JPEG XL holds 16-bit samples natively where WebP does not, and
pins the float refusal, which is the one carrier vips writes and libviprs does
not.

COUNTERPART_REV stays where it is until the core PR merges and there is a SHA
to pin.
